My Design Philosophy

All design is fundamentally about solving problems. Before anything takes shape, I, as the designer, need to be as familiar with the core problem as the people affected by it. A solution is only as strong as the questions that lead to it, so I never let data, anecdotes, or assumptions tell the full story alone. I think in systems. Every product is comprised of moving parts before, during, and after someone interacts with it. To create a truly watertight design solution is to create a product that continues moving with the user even after handoff.

I believe that function follows form. In a landscape where everything can be shared across platforms at breakneck speeds, perception of one universal product can often shape individual realities. As such, I stay critical, work fast, and ensure that reality backs imagination and not the other way around. Whether the result is something to be held or tapped, the philosophy stays the same. A product must solve a problem, and with ever more importance, it must look like it solves that problem first.
